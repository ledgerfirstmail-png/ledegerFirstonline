Call Out Buddy

The employee call-out verification system that turns chaos into control.

The Problem

When an employee calls out, you're stuck asking: Did they actually report it? Who did they tell? Is it documented anywhere? And by the time you figure it out — it's already caused disruption.

The Solution

The Call Out Buddy handles everything automatically. Employees submit a quick online form from a QR code. We verify every call-out with a confirmation call — no fake submissions, no miscommunication, no "I thought I told someone" excuses. Every call-out is automatically recorded, time-stamped, and logged in a clean spreadsheet. Managers and key staff get instant email notifications. The right people know immediately so you can act fast and adjust coverage.
